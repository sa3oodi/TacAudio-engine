TacAudio is installed. One-time setup (about 1 minute) - the Setup assistant
opens on first launch and walks you through it:

1. Choose your headphones.

2. Click "Install engine". Windows asks for administrator permission once,
   and your sound pauses for a few seconds while Windows audio restarts.
   TacAudio's engine now runs inside Windows audio on your headphones -
   no virtual cable needed.

3. Set up your game:
   - Audio output: your headphones (or the Windows default).
   - In the game's audio settings choose 7.1 / Surround / Home theater,
     and turn off the game's own 3D-headphone mode.

4. Start the game. The Setup assistant shows "Your game is sending 7.1"
   when everything works.

Hotkeys: Ctrl+Alt+F10 turns TacAudio on/off, Ctrl+Alt+F9 switches 3D on/off.
Closing the window keeps TacAudio in the tray; right-click the tray icon to exit.

Optional: if Direct mode can't be used on your PC, the Setup assistant's
VB-CABLE tab guides you through the older virtual-cable setup.

Help and updates: https://github.com/sa3oodi/TacAudio-engine
